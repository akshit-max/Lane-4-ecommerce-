Section 02

Pricing, GST & UPI Verification

All prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable GST (12% on racing swimwear/apparel under HSN 6112; 18% on goggles, caps, training gear, and shipping under HSN 9004/6506/9506 and SAC 9968).

  • Manual UPI QR Settlement: Every UPI payment requires a valid, unique 12-digit numeric UTR/reference number and a clear payment confirmation screenshot.

  • Stock Reservation: Inventory is reserved on order creation and confirmed upon payment verification; unverified or rejected orders are cancelled and inventory is released.
